# Road to the Trophy V3 Promotion UX Review V2.15

The reviewed public experience presents a single Road to the Trophy scenario and loads V3 directly from the canonical engine artifact. There is no visible engine selector, candidate label, legacy scenario, or comparison prompt. SimuAI Tournament Engine V3 and the 50,000 complete simulations are stated once in the introduction, followed by a concise explanation of group variability and head-to-head knockout simulation.

The Tournament Atlas visual structure is preserved. Users can still pan, zoom, focus groups and rounds, select teams, inspect matches, and filter statuses. Match details retain a simple explanation of why one side is favorite and why the opponent can still advance. The responsive layout uses the existing Atlas styles and introduces no new fixed-width comparison panel. The remaining projected-bracket limitation is visible but does not create a competing scenario.
