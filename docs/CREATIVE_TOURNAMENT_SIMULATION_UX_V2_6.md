# Creative Tournament Simulation UX V2.6

The simulation page keeps the established cards, tabs and probability table,
then adds three focused layers:

1. A live context strip showing simulations, finished scores locked and future
   fixtures simulated.
2. Movement cards showing the largest qualification rises and falls versus
   the original V2.4 simulation.
3. A Projected Campaign section with a proxy leader, top contenders and a
   short group-to-qualification timeline.

The proxy is not called an official winner probability. A visible limitation
explains that no knockout bracket or final pairing exists in the available
data. Group exploration remains the detailed core; creative elements stay
above it as compact narrative summaries.
