# Road to the Trophy Public Language V2.15

Public language now describes the experience rather than its migration state. The principal terms are Road to the Trophy, Scénario SimuAI, Projection du tournoi, Chemin projeté, and Moteur tournoi SimuAI. Labels such as candidate, under review, comparator, old scenario, current versus V3, and legacy simulation have been removed from the main screen.

Method language remains short and useful: groups vary in each simulation, and elimination matches are simulated head-to-head from measurable available strengths. Match inspection includes a plain-language “Pourquoi ?” explanation, the favorite’s measurable advantages, the outsider’s remaining route to an upset, and unavailable context. Detailed model terminology and historical comparisons remain in documentation rather than crowding the public journey.
