# Match Probability Engine V3 Backtest V2.14

The candidate was evaluated chronologically on the final 30% of the 1311-match expanded tournament history, with earlier matches used for rolling ratings and form only. Results: V3 log loss 1.020, Brier 0.613, accuracy 0.497; Elo baseline log loss 1.048. Group and knockout segments are published in JSON. The old Road to the Trophy formula is not a historical direct-match model and cannot be compared honestly. The candidate is not promoted because calibration should be broadened before active use.
