# Road to the Trophy Interactive Strategy V2.13.1B

## Objective

Turn `/simulation` from a static bracket into an explorable tournament story. A user can start with any of the 12 groups, select a team, follow its projected route, inspect a matchup, filter statuses, change rounds, and reset the experience.

## Product rules

- The active prediction model and match cards remain unchanged.
- Real, projected, and to-confirm states are always distinct.
- No official knockout bracket is claimed before official fixtures and assignment rules exist.
- The 50,000 simulations power the experience; the alternative model remains lab-only.
- Desktop shows dense tournament context while mobile uses selectors and one round at a time.

## Success criteria

The page exposes all 12 groups, the projected 31 knockout matchups, a clearly marked third-place placeholder, team-path highlighting, match details, status filtering, round and group selectors, and a global reset.
